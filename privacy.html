Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Information we collect

When the owner authorizes the App, it receives an access token and basic account information (such as the account ID and display name) from TikTok or Instagram. This is used only to publish the owner's own content.

How information is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The App has no server database and does not collect information about other people.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any information with third parties.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings. To request deletion, contact the address below; locally stored tokens will be deleted.
